# SnKillEffects

Cosmetic kill effects for your PvP server. When a player gets a kill, their active effect plays at the victim's death location: purely visual, never damaging. Players own effects individually, and pick their active one from a paginated menu that shows the whole catalogue with each entry marked available or not available.

## Features

- **15 effects out of the box**: Lightning, Totem, Warden, TNT, Fire Ring, Anvil, Rocket, Blood Splash, Soul Escape, Ender Void, Skull Trophy, Firework Show, Shockwave, Ice Shatter and Dragon Roar.
- **Nothing is ever damaged.** No effect deals damage, sets fire, knocks back or changes a block. The falling anvil places nothing and the fireworks cannot hurt bystanders.
- **Built for a store.** Grant an effect from console, to an online or offline player, permanently or for a limited time. Timed grants expire on their own.
- **Random mode.** A player can let the plugin roll a different owned effect on every kill.
- **Preview before you buy.** Players can play any effect at their own location, including ones they do not own yet, so the catalogue sells itself.
- **Owner-tunable.** Every particle count, radius, duration, sound, cooldown and chance lives in `effects.yml`.
- **Runs on 1.20.x and 1.21.x** from the same jar.

## Optional integrations

- **PlaceholderAPI**: unlocks the `%snkilleffects_*%` placeholders for scoreboards, tab lists and menus. Without it the plugin runs normally and simply registers no placeholders.
